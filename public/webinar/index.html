Simplifying Retail Financial Reconciliation for UAE Retailers

Struggling to match sales, payments, settlements and inventory across multiple retail channels?

Explore reconSTREAM — Timestream’s end-to-end automated reconciliation platform, brought to UAE retailers by Timestream × OctAlpha.

  • Automate reconciliation across POS, e-commerce, marketplaces and payment gateways.
